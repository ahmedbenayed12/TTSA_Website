About TTSA

Tunisian Thoracic Surgery Association

Who We Are

TTSA is the Tunisian Thoracic Surgery Association, which brings together all Tunisian thoracic surgeons working in the public sector, as well as the private sector, and residents in training.

Our association became official on January 11, 2022. We have been active since March 2023.

TTSA Goals

Our core objectives and strategic mission in thoracic surgery

01
Scientific Thinking & Performance
To develop scientific thinking and professional performance in the field of thoracic surgery.
02
Scientific Relations & Advice
To work to create scientific relations and provide appropriate scientific advice in the field of the specialty.
03
Public Health Awareness
Contribute to raising public health awareness of the dangers of smoking and related diseases.
04
Patient Care Coordination
Seek to coordinate efforts to improve the care of patients requiring thoracic surgery.
05
Partnerships & Diplomatic Relations
Establish relations with associations and organizations with similar interests inside and outside the country, with which Tunisia has diplomatic relations.
