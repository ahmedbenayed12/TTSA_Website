A Word from the President
Dear Colleagues, Dear Friends, Esteemed Partners,

On behalf of the Board of the Tunisian Thoracic Surgery Association (TTSA), I am deeply honored and delighted to welcome you to the 5th edition of our National Congress of Thoracic Surgery, taking place on November 6–7, 2026, in the historic city of Sfax at the prestigious Borj Dhiafa Hotel.

This milestone 2026 edition is dedicated to international scientific exchange and interdisciplinary collaboration. It is organized in close academic synergy with our esteemed partner societies: the French Society of Thoracic and Cardiovascular Surgery (SFCTCV), the Tunisian Society of Neurology (STN), and the Association of Pulmonologists and Allergists of Central and Southern Tunisia (APACS).

Our scientific committee has designed a state-of-the-art program centered on 4 cardinal themes: Robotic Surgery and cutting-edge innovations, multidisciplinary consensus in Thymic Pathology, therapeutic advances in Endobronchial Approaches, and enhanced recovery protocols in Perioperative Care.

Featuring 5 specialized hands-on workshops for surgical and allied health professionals alongside over 75 peer-reviewed scientific communications, this congress stands as a premier forum for clinical excellence and professional development for young surgeons and senior experts alike.

I extend my heartfelt gratitude to our national and international faculty for their distinguished scientific contributions, as well as to our medical industry partners and sponsors for their steadfast and generous support.

I wish you all an intellectually inspiring, productive congress, vibrant collegiate discussions, and a wonderful stay in Sfax.

Pr. Abdessalem HENTATI
President of the TTSA

About the TTSA

The Tunisian Thoracic Surgery Association is the national learned society bringing together all Tunisian thoracic surgeons across university hospital centers, public institutions, and private practice, as well as surgical residents in training.

🏛️ Officially founded: January 11, 2022 · Fully active since March 2023
Strategic Objectives
01
Scientific Inquiry & Performance
Advance academic inquiry and continuing clinical excellence in thoracic surgery.
02
Academic Relations & Advisory
Foster academic networks and provide expert advisory opinions in the specialty.
03
Public Health Awareness
Raise public health awareness regarding smoking hazards and associated thoracic pathologies.
04
Patient Care Coordination
Coordinate collective efforts to optimize comprehensive care for thoracic surgery patients.
05
International Partnerships
Build enduring bilateral relations with peer thoracic and cardiovascular learned societies worldwide.

The majestic prayer hall of the Great Mosque of Sfax, founded in 849 AD under the Aghlabid dynasty alongside the medina's earliest ramparts. Beyond its remarkable architecture supported by a forest of antique columns, this monument stood for centuries as the beating heart of Islamic learning and theology in southern Tunisia. It was home to renowned Sfaxian scholars, jurists, and theologians—such as Qadi Ali Ibn Aslam Al-Bakri (who oversaw its construction) and the eminent scholar of law and language Ali of Sfax (Sheikh Ali Ennouri)—whose teachings and intellectual rigor profoundly shaped Islamic thought and the region's cultural identity.
